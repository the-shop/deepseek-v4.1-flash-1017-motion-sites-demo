03 — The Craft

Four steps.
No forms.

We built Atlas Wander because the best trips we'd ever taken were made by a person, not a platform. The process is still that simple.

01

The Conversation

Ninety minutes on the phone. We ask about the last trip that disappointed you before we ask where you want to go. That second answer is usually the useful one.

02

The Sketch

Inside a week you get a hand-drawn route on paper: flights, drives, the three nights we think you should cut, and the two we think you should add.

03

The Refinement

We hold the tables, charter the boats, and rebook around the rain. You get a single document and a single phone number that a human being actually answers.

04

The Departure

Eleven days out, a leather folder arrives: folded maps, a local SIM, printed addresses, and the names of the people who will be genuinely glad to see you.
