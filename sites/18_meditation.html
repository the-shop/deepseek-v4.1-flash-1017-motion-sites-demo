01 — The rhythm

Four. Seven. Eight.

The 4-7-8 pattern is old, simple and stubbornly effective. A longer exhale than inhale nudges the nervous system toward rest. Still holds the count so you don't have to.

4
Inhaleseconds

Through the nose, slow and quiet. Let the belly rise before the chest does.

7

Keep it gentle. This is a pause, not a performance — nothing to prove at the top of the breath.

Exhaleseconds

Out through the mouth, unhurried. The long exhale is the part that does the work.

One full cycle takes nineteen seconds. Do four of them and most people notice their shoulders drop before they notice anything else.

03 — Sound

Every tone is made here.

Nothing is streamed or downloaded. Still synthesises its voices live in your browser — four sine waves through a low-pass filter, each drifting against the others so that no two moments sound exactly the same.

Headphones recommended. Volume is deliberately low.
