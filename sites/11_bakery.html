How we roll

Midnight to seven, every single day

There's no shortcut for lamination. Here's what happens between the last customer of the night and the first one of the morning.

01

Wake Clementine

Our sourdough starter is six years old and lives in a crock behind the espresso machine. She gets fed at midnight, then the croissant dough gets mixed at 12:40.

02

Fold, rest, repeat

Twenty-seven folds. Three rests. One very cold walk-in. The butter has to stay at 16°C or the layers fuse and you lose the crackle entirely.

03

Doors at seven

Trays hit the case at 6:55. By 7:00 there's a line. By noon it's just crumbs, a warm coffee cup, and three regulars who know the schedule by heart.
