01 — The Build

Seven plies. No shortcuts.

Pressed
in Pittsburgh
★ 7 ply ★
"No shortcuts. Ever."

Every G&G deck starts as seven sheets of Canadian maple, stacked, glued with water-based resin and pressed for eighteen hours in a 40-ton hydraulic press we dragged out of a shuttered cabinet shop. We cut the blanks by hand, sand the rails twice, and screen the graphics in the parking lot. Some boards come out a little crooked. That's the point.
