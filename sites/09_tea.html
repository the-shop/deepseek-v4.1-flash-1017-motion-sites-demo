01

一期一会

One time, one meeting.

Kōyō holds one sitting at a time. Not because we are precious about it, but because the room is four and a half tatami, and the light through the maple only comes this way once a year.

We do not ask you to be quiet. The room asks on our behalf.

There is no menu to study and no choice to make. On Monday we walk the hill, taste what the season has given, and that is the tea. Everything else — the bowl, the sweet, the silence — follows from it.

What we can promise is this: the bowl in your hands will never be poured for anyone else, in exactly this way, again.

Hayashi Sōen, host

02

茶の湯

The week's tea

Four teas live in the cupboard this month. You will not choose one — the season picked it on Monday, and we will pour it at the door.

一 濃茶 Koicha Thick tea — Uji matcha, first harvest, stone-milled for two hours ¥4,800 → 二 薄茶 Usucha Thin tea — whisked to a fine foam, drunk in three and a half sips ¥3,200 → 三 煎茶 Sencha Leaf tea — hand-picked Asahi cultivar, steeped at 70°C ¥2,600 → 四 焙茶 Hōjicha Roasted stems — the tea we pour when the rain arrives ¥2,200 →

※ Every bowl comes with one wagashi, made that morning by the confectioner on the corner. She reads the weather before she reads the order.

03

四畳半

Four and a half tatami

The room was a storage shed for a hundred years. We took out the floor, put in a hearth, and then stopped — because everything else was already there.

光 i

Light 光

Washi screens dyed with persimmon tannin. At four in the afternoon in November, the room turns the exact colour of the tea.

音 ii

Sound 音

A bamboo spout fills a stone basin. The kettle sings in three pitches; we only listen for the last one.

庭 iii

Garden 庭

A borrowed view: three maples, a floor of moss, and a fence built low enough to keep the mountain in the picture.

炉 iv

Hearth 炉

A sunken ro, forty-six centimetres square. Charcoal from Kishu, laid in a fan. It will outlast every conversation held above it.

秋は窓から入ってくる

Autumn does not arrive at the gate. It enters through the window, and sits down without being asked.

Kōyō — house note, 1994

04

作法

Ninety minutes

A sitting runs a little over an hour. The clock is not part of the ritual — but it is honest about how long silence can be held.

00
入

Arrival. You leave your shoes, and then your name, at the door. Below the threshold the room is only four steps wide.

10
清

Purification. A folded cloth, a bowl of water, the hands of the person sitting beside you.

25
点

The whisking. Ninety seconds of wrist and nothing else. The bowl is turned twice before it reaches you.

45
飲

The bowl. You drink from the side facing you. The last sip is always the loudest thing in the room.

70
静

The room. Nobody speaks. Rain, if we are lucky. Otherwise the kettle, and the maples doing their work outside.

90
出

Departure. The gate closes. The maples carry on without you, which is the point of the whole arrangement.
