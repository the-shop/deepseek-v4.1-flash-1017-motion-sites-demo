01 — Studio Since 2011 · 14 people

Kinetik is a motion studio for brands that refuse to sit still. We design the exact moment a product stops being a screen and starts feeling alive — the easing, the weight, the pause before the punchline. Everything we ship is measured in frames, and remembered in feeling.

03 — Capabilities Five disciplines

What we do

01

Brand films

↗

Concept to final grade. We storyboard, animate and score launch films that carry a brand's rhythm into every frame — from 6-second social cuts to 90-second hero pieces.

02

Interaction design

↗

Micro-interactions, page choreography and scroll systems. We prototype in code, not just in Figma, so what you approve is what your engineers can actually ship.

03

3D & CGI

↗

Procedural modelling, physical materials and real-time WebGL. Product renders that behave like objects rather than photographs, running at 60fps in the browser.

04

Sound design

↗

Half of motion is audio. We compose UI sonics, sonic logos and full scores in-house — tuned to the same easing curves as the visuals.

05

Identity systems

↗

Motion guidelines, design tokens and animation libraries. A living system your team can extend for years without losing the pulse.

04 — Process Four movements

How we move

Movement 01

Listen

Two weeks of questions before a single frame. We interview your team, your users and your competitors, then write the feeling we're aiming for in one sentence.

DiscoveryAuditPositioning
Movement 02

Blueprint

Every project gets a motion principle: a tiny rule that decides how things enter, settle and leave. From that we build timing scales, easing tokens and a storyboard.

StyleframesMotion tokensStoryboard
Movement 03

Animate

This is where we live. Design and code sit in the same room, so the easing you approve is the easing that ships — no translation loss, no "we'll do it in dev".

3D / CGIPrototypesSound
Movement 04

Hand off

Documented components, Lottie and WebGL exports, and a workshop with your engineers. We stay on call for six weeks after launch to tune what real usage reveals.
