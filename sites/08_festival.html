02

The Grounds

Two hundred acres of oak, dust and meadow. Everything is walkable. Nothing is paved.

01

The Sun Stage

A 40-metre steel arch facing the western ridge. Built to catch the last hour of light and hold it for the headline set.

02

The Undertow

Down in the creek bowl, where the ground is cool and the sound bounces off thirty metres of standing oak.

03

Neon Grove

Twelve hundred hand-wound bulbs strung through the trees. Runs until the sun tells it not to.

04

The Long Table

One continuous table, 80 metres long, shared by whoever sits down. Wood-fired food from six regional kitchens.

03

Schedule

Four days, sunrise to sunrise. The closing set ends at 06:30 on the longest day of the year.

04
