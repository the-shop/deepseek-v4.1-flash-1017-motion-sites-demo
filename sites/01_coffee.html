01 — The roast

We roast in six-kilo batches over white oak, chasing the exact second the sugar turns to smoke. Then we stop. Everything after that is ash.

Our drum runs at 214°C and we log every curve by hand, on paper, with a pencil that lives taped to the roaster. If a batch doesn't crack twice and cool in under four minutes, it doesn't leave the building.

04 — The ritual

Brew it right the first time.

Four steps. About four minutes. Good enough to make you late for something, worth it anyway.

  • 01

    Weigh

    18 g of coffee, 288 g of water. A cheap scale will beat an expensive grinder every single time.

    →
  • 02

    Grind

    Medium-fine, somewhere near sea salt. If it looks like dust on the counter, you're about to drink silt.

    →
  • 03

    Bloom

    Pour 50 g and wait forty seconds. The bed should rise, fall, and settle like a slow breath.

    →
  • 04

    Pour

    Two slow spirals, finishing at 3:30. If it runs fast, grind finer tomorrow. If it stalls, loosen up.

    →
