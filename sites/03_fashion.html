01

WE CUT IN silence.

NOIR/SS27 is twenty-four garments and one rule: the body is not corrected, it is framed.

Every piece begins as a flat plane — bonded wool, silk faille, raw cotton canvas, horsehair — held under weights for seventy-two hours before a single blade touches it. We do not drape. We subtract.

What remains is a silhouette that refuses the waist. Volume is drawn from the seam alone: zero darts, zero fusible, zero apology.

Origin

Atelier 07, Rue de Turenne, Paris 3ᵉ. Sixth floor, no lift.

Method

Zero darts. All volume is carried by the seam and the weight of the cloth.

Material

Bonded wool, silk faille, raw canvas, horsehair, horn.

03 — ANATOMY / VOID COAT
01

Shoulder

A fourteen-centimetre drop. The seam is set behind the arm so the shoulder reads as a single, unresolved plane — never a corner.

02

Lapel

One peak, twelve centimetres, pressed flat. No interfacing, no canvas — the bonded wool is expected to hold itself, or to fail honestly.

03

Closure

Three horn buttons. The fourth is a ghost: stitched, non-functional, present only so the eye finds symmetry it can rest in.

04

Hem

Finished by hand in a three-centimetre blind stitch. No fusible tape. The coat should fall, not stand. It is the last thing we do and the first thing you notice.
