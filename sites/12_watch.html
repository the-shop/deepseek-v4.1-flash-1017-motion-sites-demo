01 — Manifesto

One thousand nine hundred and seventy-four hours of hand-finishing, folded into forty-one millimetres of rose gold. The Meridian is not worn to tell the time. It is worn to tell you something about the time you keep.

03 — Calibre M-71

A movement
you can hear
from across
the room.

A column-wheel chronograph, hand-wound, built on 218 components in our Vallée de Joux workshop. The M-71 beats at 28,800 vibrations per hour — four hertz, eight beats a second — regulated by a free-sprung balance with four gold inertia weights and no regulator to drift.

04 — The Hand

Three textures.
One pair of hands.

Dial

Guilloché

Seven hundred concentric rings, cut on a 1919 rose engine and never polished out. The pattern shifts as the wrist turns — it is the only part of the watch that moves without a gear.

Mainplate

Perlage

Overlapping circles of overlapping circles, applied freehand with a rotating abrasive peg. It hides nothing and forgives nothing: a single skipped spot and the plate is scrapped.

Bridges

Côtes de Genève

Parallel waves drawn across solid rose gold with a single pass of the file. Each bridge takes forty minutes. We make eleven a day, and we stop when the light goes.
