The Society · 02

A room, a board,
and no clocks.

We meet above Goldsboro Books on Cecil Court — the narrow street that has sold secondhand print in London since the seventeenth century. There is one long table, eleven boards, and a kettle that has not been switched off since 2011.

The club is capped at forty-two members so that everyone gets a game on the night they turn up. We do not play for ratings. We do not play for money. We play because a well-made combination is the only kind of argument that can be won twice.

Newcomers are welcome on Tuesdays. Bring nothing but an interest in the beautiful move.

  • i. No clocks, ever.
  • ii. Say the move, then play it.
  • iii. Analysis after the game — never during.
  • iv. Bring one game a month to share.
