02 — The Engine

Analog behaviour, modelled to the last drifting capacitor.

We didn't sample a vintage synth and call it a day. Pulse-9 models the circuit — every transistor, every tolerance, every degree of thermal drift — then lets you push it further than the original ever could.

01

Twin oscillators + discrete sub

Saw, square and triangle shapes with continuously variable detune up to 28 cents. A separate sub-oscillator an octave down keeps the low end planted, even when the filter is wide open.

02

24 dB transistor ladder filter

A four-pole ladder that self-oscillates from nine o'clock onward. Zero-latency feedback keeps the transient of your first note intact — the part most digital filters quietly eat.

03

16-slot modulation matrix

Route any source to any destination from the front panel. Two LFOs, three envelopes, velocity and channel aftertouch — all assignable without a screen dive.
