01 — THE SIGNAL

A dying arcade.
One stray photon.

The cabinet is forgetting itself. Corridors fold, colours bleed, and the last working light has just enough charge for one more run.

Lumen Drift is a one-thumb arcade odyssey built on a single idea: your light always lags behind your hand. Every corridor is a memory the machine is trying to delete. Slip through the gaps, collect the motes, and keep moving — because standing still is how a photon dies.

02 — MECHANICS

Four rules of the drift.

RULE / 01

Steer the lag

Your light trails half a beat behind your cursor. That delay is the whole game — plan the gap, not the pixel.

RULE / 02

Pixel & glow

Chunky 4px blocks on the way in. Blooming light on the way out. Two art styles fighting for the same screen.

RULE / 03

Phase shift

Fill the meter, hold the button, and the world drops to wireframe. Obstacles become suggestions for two seconds.

RULE / 04

Runs, not saves

No checkpoints. A run is ninety seconds of nerve. Your best score stays burned onto the cabinet.

04 — SOUND & RELEASE

Analog synths,
broken cabinets.

Twelve tracks of tape-saturated synthwave, recorded through a failing arcade PCB by Mira Voss. The soundtrack tracks your run in real time — push faster and the arpeggio opens up. Die, and the whole mix drops a semitone and refuses to come back until you do.

Mastered for headphones. Mixed for one more attempt at 2am.
